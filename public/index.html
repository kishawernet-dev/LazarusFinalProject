Directive 01

1. Outbreak Diagnosis

Diagnosis Lead: What is the Lazarus Virus doing to the body? Include at least two pieces of evidence from genetics, cells, organelles, macromolecules, immune response, ATP loss, or lactic acid buildup.

Directive 02

2. Ecosystem Damage Report

Ecology Lead: What happened to the ecosystem after the outbreak? Use at least one graph or data reference from food webs, nutrient cycles, biodiversity, symbiosis, or succession.

Directive 03

3. Restoration Blueprint

Restoration Lead: What should Genesis Global Command do first, next, and long-term? Include a short-term action, a first-month action, and a long-term recovery action.

Directive 04

4. Individual Scientific Defense

Everyone: Explain your role, the evidence you used, why it matters, and one limitation or weakness in the plan.
